## The game (design spec — re-author it, do not copy old code)
2D top-down open-world crime game, plain ES modules + canvas, no build step.
Player: Rico Vale owes $12,000 to fixer Marisol Vega. Ten-mission story arc, debt
paid by mission payouts. Free roam after the story. localStorage save/continue.
World 3600x2600. Roads: vertical x = 300,700,1100,1500,1900,2300,2700,3100;
horizontal y = 200,600,1000,1400,1800,2200; road width 64. World bounds clamp
player to x 300..3400, y 180..2440. Seeded RNG (mulberry32) so scenes are reproducible.

Districts (rect + ground color + accent):
- Downtown (300,200,1400x800, #3d4450, #7fb2ff, tall buildings)
- Harbor (2400,200,1200x800, #46525a, #6fd3e0)
- Beach (300,1900,3300x700, #c9b183, #ffcf5e)
- Old Town (300,1100,1400x700, #5a4a3c, #e0a06f)
- Suburbs (1800,1100,1800x700, #4a5d43, #9fd08a)
- NEW Airport (1750,200,600x600, #4a4d55, #ffd94a) — tarmac. Runway strip x 1800..2300,
  y 380..500 with white center dashes and threshold marks; terminal building at
  (1800,220,240x120); control tower at (2280,240,60x60); a parked passenger plane
  landmark at (2050,440) — white fuselage, teal stripe, wings, tail fin. Draw the
  runway AFTER the roads so it reads as tarmac crossing them.
Landmarks (keep clear of generated buildings): Solmar Mall (820,520,260x160),
Bay Stadium (1120,1420,360x220), Lighthouse (3300,330,120x120, sweeping beam),
Marina (2820,620,300x180, docks). Water: east strip (3360,180,240x860) and south
strip (280,2400,3320x200). Buildings: one per road-block cell with jitter, skip
roads/water/landmark plots, ~16% empty lots/parks, district-tinted palettes.
Spawn: (950,1000). Marina (2960,700); boat spot (3380,560).
20 hidden sun coins (+$25, +1 cred each), 14 health/armor pickups (respawn 25s).
5 cooldown spots (payphones/alleys) that drain wanted faster when the player hides there.

Missions (10, payouts sum to exactly $12,000 — the debt):
1. First Errand $500 — goto Harbor warehouse (2900,800), then goto Downtown drop (1000,760).
2. Wheels Up $700 — steal a sports car (walk up, press E), then goto Old Town garage (1450,1250) in it.
3. The Collector $900 — collect 3 beach packages (700,2050), (1800,2100), (2900,2050).
4. Hot Wheels $1000 — street race: 5 gates around Downtown, 75 seconds (press R at the flag).
5. Heat Wave $1100 — raise wanted to 2 stars, then lose the cops back to 0.
6. Taxi Nights $1200 — complete 2 taxi fares.
7. Harbor Lights $1300 — take the boat from the marina to the lighthouse buoy (3420,470), then sail back to the marina (2960,700).
8. Stadium Standoff $1400 — defeat 4 police at Bay Stadium (1300,1530).
9. NEW Runway Heist $1700 — pick up air cargo at the parked plane (2050,440), then deliver it to the Old Town garage (1450,1250). Dialogue: Marisol smuggled cargo on a night flight; the plane is on the runway.
10. NEW The Getaway $2200 — finale: raise wanted to 3 stars, lose the cops to 0, then bring the final payment to Marisol Downtown (1000,760). Debt hits $0 — "DEBT PAID" screen, free roam continues.
Each mission: banner + 3-line dialogue (MARISOL/RICO), step labels shown under the
banner and as a pulsing marker on the map/minimap. Mission-complete card shows pay
and remaining debt; Enter/click continues. Pause screen shows mission x/10.
Step kinds to implement: goto (with optional needCar), collect, steal, race, heat,
cool, fares, boat, defeat (counts cops destroyed since the mission started).

Vehicles (top speed / accel): sedan 430/340, taxi 420/330, pickup 400/300,
sports 620/520, van 360/260, bus 320/200, police 560/460, boat 420/300,
NEW motorcycle 660/560 (w 34, h 16, color #e8a13c, label 'Motorcycle' — fast, nimble,
drawn narrower with a rider dot). Parked cars: 16 spots incl. 2 motorcycles
((900,640) and (2500,1440)); the boat waits at the marina. Stealing a parked car
gives +1 wanted star and counts as a stolen car. Traffic: 18 cars driving the roads
(they brake for cars ahead, wrap around the map edges, rare U-turns). Pedestrians:
30 walkers who flee from gunfire. Entering/exiting with E; Space = handbrake/sprint.

Wanted 0–5 stars: shooting, stealing, speeding near police, and destroying police
cars raise it. Police spawn up to floor(wanted) patrol cars that chase, ram, and
shoot; roadblocks at 3+ stars, spike strips (flat tires 5s) at 5 stars, police
helicopter with searchlight at 4+ stars. Wanted decays when no police are near
(faster inside a payphone/alley radius). Dying (0 HP) respawns at the Downtown
clinic with -10% cash, wanted cleared.

Combat: mouse aim + click to shoot (pistol). Bullets damage police/traffic cars and
can destroy them (explosion, +2 cred, +heat). Player has HP 100 + armor 100 (armor
absorbs 70%). Health/armor pickups heal.
Side activities (always available): taxi fares (drive a taxi, a fare appears nearby,
pick up then drop off for $60+ by distance), courier deliveries (a package spawns
every ~14s, deliver for $50+ by distance), street races (R at the flag, 5 gates,
75s, $150 + time bonus), sun coins, races won / cars stolen / cops down tracked.
Economy: cash, debt, street cred (missions +15, fares +3, deliveries +2, cops +2,
coins +1, race win +8). Save on mission complete + coin pickup; Continue on title.

NEW day/night cycle: clock starts 10:00, a full day lasts 6 minutes of play.
Phases: day 08–17 (no tint), dusk 17–19 (warm orange tint), night 19–06 (dark blue
tint, building windows glow brighter, vehicle headlight cones + player headlight),
dawn 06–08 (soft pink tint). HUD clock top-right under the cred line, e.g. "DAY 1 · 18:24"
with a sun/moon glyph by phase. Screenshot scenes set an explicit time (below).

Audio: WebAudio synth only, no audio files — shoot, crash, explosion, pickup, coin,
mission start/done, fare, denied, engine hum (pitch with speed), two-tone siren,
NEW plane flyby whoosh when the player enters the Airport district (20s cooldown).
M toggles mute. Sound starts only after user input (AudioContext resume).

HUD + minimap: minimap top-left (196x142) with district names (incl. Airport),
water, mission marker (pulsing), police (red dots), helicopter, taxi marker, player
(white dot). Wanted stars top-right (filled gold/red, empty dim). Cash/debt/cred
top-right. HP/armor bars bottom-left. Speed + vehicle bottom-right. Objective
banner top-center, current step under it, race timer, toast messages, dialogue box,
crosshair. Title screen: city backdrop, SOLMAR BAY, "Rico Vale owes $12,000 to fixer
Marisol Vega. Ten missions. One way out.", controls, NEW GAME (Enter) / CONTINUE (C)
buttons. Pause (P/Esc), dead screen, complete screen ("DEBT PAID — SOLMAR BAY IS YOURS",
free roam continues).

Screenshot mode: `?shot=<name>&seed=<n>` stages a fixed scene, no title screen,
`window.__shotReady = true` after 5 rendered frames. Four scenes (seed 7):
- chase: mission 2 live, sports car (1100,800) heading north, wanted 3, 3 police
